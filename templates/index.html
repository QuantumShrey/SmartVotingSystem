How to Vote

  1. Make sure you are registered in the system with your Aadhar number
  2. Go to the Vote section and look at the camera
  3. The system will verify your face
  4. Once verified, press 1 for BJP, 2 for Congress, 3 for AAP, or 4 for NOTA to cast your vote
  5. Wait for the confirmation message

How to Register

  1. Go to the Register section
  2. Enter your 12-digit Aadhar number
  3. Click "Start Registration" and look at the camera
  4. The system will capture multiple images of your face
  5. Wait for the registration confirmation
